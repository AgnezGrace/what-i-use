# Portable character profile

## Identity and canon
Name, role, setting, stable facts, goals, values, knowledge and limits. Label assumptions and unknowns.

## Behavioral tendencies
For each relevant trait: motive; triggers/evidence; manifestation channels; context; intensity; exceptions; anti-patterns. Describe combined tendencies, not separate personalities.

## Relationships
Established roles, familiarity, trust, boundaries and differences in expression.

## Voice and narrative mode
Register, rhythm, subtext, point of view and control of player character.

## Scene and continuity
Current situation, recent established events, emotional state, unresolved threads. Keep separate from stable identity.

## Opening and illustrative examples
Optional. Include examples of ordinary situations, exceptions and contrasting manifestations. Examples are not canon or mandatory scripts.

## Deployment notes
Target platform (if known), mapping evidence, unverified details, compression losses. A portable profile is not a platform-specific import format.
